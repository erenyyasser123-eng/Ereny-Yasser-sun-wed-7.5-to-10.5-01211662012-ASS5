A Personal Story - From Financial Chaos to Control

Your Journey: From Paycheck to Paycheck to Financial Freedom

Imagine this scenario: You’re a hardworking employee earning a decent salary every month. But here’s the problem – by the 10th day of each month, your bank account is nearly empty. 🤯

Sound familiar? You get your salary on the 1st, feel rich for a few days, spend on everything you want, and then realize you have nothing left to survive the rest of the month. The cycle repeats every single month.

The Solution:

You decide enough is enough! It’s time to take control of your finances. You’re going to build “Mudabbir” – your personal finance management dashboard that will help you track every penny and finally break the paycheck-to-paycheck cycle.

Here’s a breakdown of the key sections you’ll be building:

1

Overall Layout & Sidebar Navigation:

A clean, organized interface where you can easily navigate between different financial tools and reports. This sidebar will be your command center for all money management activities.

2

Header/Top Bar:

Shows your profile, notifications about upcoming bills, and the Mudabbir logo. This keeps you connected and informed about important financial alerts.

3

Summary Cards:

Three key metrics at a glance – your Current Balance (how much you have right now), monthly Income (what comes in), and Expenses (what goes out). These cards will show percentage changes to help you track your progress.

4

Recent Activities & Transactions:

A detailed log of where your money went. Every coffee purchase, grocery bill, and online subscription will be tracked here so you can see your spending patterns clearly.

5

Monthly Bills Tracker:

Never miss a bill payment again! This section shows all your upcoming payments with due dates and amounts, helping you plan ahead and avoid late fees.

6

Budget Overview:

Set budgets for different categories (food, entertainment, transport) and track how much you have left to spend. Visual progress bars will show you when you’re getting close to your limits.

7

Income vs. Expenses Chart:

A visual representation of your weekly income versus expenses. This chart will help you identify problem weeks and successful saving periods.

8

Payment Methods:

Manage all your cards and payment accounts in one place. Track which card you used for what purchase and monitor your credit card limits.

9

Account Summary:

A comprehensive view of all your accounts – checking, savings, and credit cards. See all your balances and limits in one organized section.

Learning Goals

Assignment Objectives

This assignment will test your ability to build a complex, real-world financial dashboard layout using modern CSS. You will translate a design built with a utility-first framework (Tailwind CSS) into clean, vanilla CSS, focusing on CSS Grid, Flexbox, responsive design, and custom properties.

Learning Outcomes

Build a complete, responsive financial dashboard using only HTML and vanilla CSS, demonstrating mastery of CSS Grid and Flexbox for layout, media queries for responsiveness, and a solid understanding of the box model, custom properties, and semantic HTML.

Implementing Modern UI Components

Recreate various UI components such as progress bars, cards with shadows, and styled tables. This will involve applying advanced CSS properties for shadows, borders, and background gradients to match the design.

Mastering CSS Grid and Flexbox for Layout

Implement various layouts, from the main page structure to individual card components, using both CSS Grid and Flexbox. You will use properties like 'display: grid', 'grid-template-columns', 'display: flex', 'flex-direction', 'justify-content', and 'align-items'.

Responsive Design with Media Queries

Write media queries to create a fully responsive design that adapts to different screen sizes, from mobile to desktop. You will practice adjusting layouts, font sizes, and spacing for different breakpoints to ensure optimal viewing on all devices.

Using CSS Custom Properties for Theming

Define and utilize CSS custom properties (variables) to manage colors, font sizes, and spacing throughout the dashboard. This will enhance maintainability and allow for easy theming adjustments in the future.

Styling with Pseudo-classes and Pseudo-elements

Enhance the user interface with pseudo-classes like ':hover', ':focus', and ':active' for interactive effects, and pseudo-elements like '::before' and '::after' for decorative elements, icons, or subtle visual enhancements.

Creating Smooth Transitions and Animations

Apply 'transition' effects for smooth hover animations and use 'transform' to create subtle visual effects on elements. You will also implement a 3D flip effect for credit cards using CSS transforms.
